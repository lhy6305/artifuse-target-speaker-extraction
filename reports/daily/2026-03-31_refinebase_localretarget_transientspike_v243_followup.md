## Read

- This continuation is clearly real.
  The stronger selector is active at the intended smaller scale:
  `overlap_dual train 13 / 233, val 2 / 67`.
- But the branch now shows the wrong qualitative surface for this family goal.
  Relative
  `v240`,
  all four non-blocker checks improve strongly
  (`+1.1033 / +0.5231 / +0.7379 / +0.5672 dB`),
  while the active blocker gives back
  `-0.5148 dB`.
- So this is not "repair the mixed near-real candidate more selectively".
  It is "push the split-route family toward a safer keep-dominant regime by spending most of the blocker surplus".
- The targeted near-real probes confirm that the direction is wrong.
  Relative
  `v240`,
  both
  `friend_raw`
  and
  `guodegang_raw`
  move negative on the speech probe,
  and the focused
  `guodegang`
  transient probe is also negative.
- This makes the scientific contrast with
  `v242`
  sharper:
  a broader transient-heavy retarget was safer but near-tie;
  a stricter transient-only retarget crosses into real regression.

## Conclusion

- `v243` is a bounded reject on top of
  `v240`.
- Do not keep narrowing this same local-selector-retarget family only by stronger transient thresholds.
- `v242` remains the only non-reject point on this selector-retarget line,
  and it still stayed only practical tie on the targeted near-real probes.
- If this family continues,
  the next subgroup should not be "more transient-only".
  It should add a different discriminator,
  not just stronger transient concentration.
